Filtration Principles

How Purification Works

A multi-stage journey engineered to remove dissolved and suspended contaminants while preserving taste.

1
Pre-Filtration

Raw tap or groundwater passes through a high-density spun polypropylene cartridge to trap mud, rust, and silt.

2
Carbon Adsorption

Activated carbon block absorbs volatile organic compounds, foul chlorine odor, and harmful pesticide traces.

3
Membrane & UV Barrier

0.0001 micron RO membrane strips heavy metals, followed by UV disinfection neutralizing bacteria and viruses.

4
Mineral Restoration

TDS balancer replenishes essential natural minerals before dispensing into an airtight antibacterial reservoir.

Exact filtration stages vary by product model and input water source.
